Abuse / neglect type

Select one or more. The arrow runs from From person toward To person.

Family and emotional connections can coexist. Choosing an emotional relationship on a marriage adds a separate line and keeps the marriage and children.

Connect an existing child to a couple

A person can have multiple marriages. Use From person and To person to connect existing symbols. Choose an existing connector to change its endpoints or type. For stepfamilies, keep the child's biological couple connection and add a separate Stepparent–child connection if wanted. Moving a child affects only their couple-based parent line, not other relationships.

6. Marker display & custom markers

Each selected condition gets a section: solid = active, partial = remission, hatching = suspected. Up to four conditions use quadrants; additional conditions use smaller grid sections. Custom conditions are solid. Colors follow your supplied reference, with the legend defining them.
