Click a shape to flip to its details. Drag a shape to move it; drag text independently.

People & symbols

Text appearance & position

Drag any name or date independently of its symbol. Font settings apply to the selected person's labels; click Apply changes to save them.

Connect people

Choose From person and To person, then Apply connector. Or use Select on diagram and click two people in order. For a couple's child: select the couple's family line, choose “Child of selected couple,” then select the child. Drag people to reposition. Select a line to delete it.
