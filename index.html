Connector shape & bends

Select a connector on the diagram or choose it in Quick connect.

Drag the connector itself or its blue bend handle to reshape it independently of people. Ends stay attached to people, and the line follows them when moved. Twins and multiples move their shared birth fork together. Apply bend to sibling connections moves the shared family bar.

Households

Group people who live together. A person can belong to more than one home. Family connections stay unchanged.

People living in this home

Apply each household before saving progress. Outlines follow their members when moved. Arrange separate homes apart on the diagram to keep their boundaries clear. Shared-home members may have overlapping outlines.
